# BuildSphere

**AI-Powered Home Design, Engineering & Construction Platform**

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Product Type:** Mobile App (iOS & Android) + Web Platform + Professional Portal + Admin Dashboard

---

## Executive Summary

BuildSphere is an end-to-end AI-powered residential construction platform that enables users to design, visualize, budget, engineer, permit, and manage the construction of custom homes from concept to completion.

Rather than acting as a simple floor plan generator, BuildSphere functions as a complete digital ecosystem that connects homeowners, architects, engineers, surveyors, contractors, suppliers, lenders, and permitting agencies through one intelligent platform.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, BIM (Building Information Modeling), photorealistic rendering, construction estimation, permit management, and project collaboration into a unified experience.

---

## Vision

Become the world's leading digital platform for designing and building custom homes.

---

## Mission

Allow anyone to build a professionally designed home by making architecture, engineering, permitting, budgeting, and construction management accessible through AI while keeping licensed professionals responsible for work requiring professional judgment and approval.
